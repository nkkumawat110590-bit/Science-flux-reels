• Series LCR: Z = √(R² + (X_L − X_C)²), tan φ = (X_L − X_C)/R (φ > 0: current lags); rms voltages add as phasors, not as numbers

• Average power P = V_rms I_rms cos φ (sinusoidal steady state), power factor cos φ = R/Z (series circuit), wattless current I sin φ; choke coil
• Ideal LC: ω₀ = 1/√(LC), energy q²/2C + ½Li² constant (no resistance), analogy with spring-block SHM
• AC generator ε₀ = NBAω (axis ⊥ uniform B, constant ω); ideal transformer V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p = I_p/I_s; efficiency, losses, high-voltage transmission (loss ∝ 1/V² at fixed power sent)
