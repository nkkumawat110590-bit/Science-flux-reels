Inside:
• Newton's laws: F_net = dp/dt (F = ma only for constant mass, inertial frame)
• Impulse J = ∫F_net dt = Δp; momentum conserved only when net external force = 0
• Atwood machine: a = (m₁ − m₂)g/(m₁ + m₂) (light string, frictionless pulley)
• Lift: N = m(g ± a) (+ for upward acceleration)
• Friction: fs ≤ μs N, fk = μk N; angle of repose tan θ = μs
• Banked road without friction: tan θ = v²/rg
