• Average translational KE of one molecule = (3/2)kT (ideal gas, thermal equilibrium)
• v_rms = √(3RT/M), v̄ = √(8RT/πM), v_mp = √(2RT/M); v_mp : v̄ : v_rms = 1 : 1.128 : 1.225 (Maxwell distribution, M in kg/mol)
• Maxwell curve, same gas: hotter → peak shifts right and gets lower, area stays 1
• Degrees of freedom f: 3 monatomic, 5 rigid diatomic, 7 with vibration, 6 rigid non-linear; equipartition (1/2)kT per quadratic term

• Mean free path λ = 1/(√2 π d² n_V) (hard spheres); Dalton's law, Graham's law, real gases (Z vs P)
