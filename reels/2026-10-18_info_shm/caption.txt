Inside:
• SHM: F = −kx, a = −ω²x (x from equilibrium, k = force constant, ω = angular frequency); x = A sin(ωt + φ), v = ω√(A² − x²) (A = amplitude, φ = initial phase)
• Phase relations, x/v/a graphs, reference circle, v–x ellipse, a–x line
• Energy E = ½kA² (no friction, no damping, PE zero at equilibrium); K and U repeat every half period, time averages E/2 each
• Spring–block T = 2π√(m/k) (ideal massless spring: horizontal, vertical or smooth incline); series 1/k = 1/k₁ + 1/k₂, parallel k₁ + k₂; cut spring kl = constant (uniform spring); massive spring 2π√((m + m_s/3)/k) (uniform spring of mass m_s, one end fixed)
• Simple pendulum T = 2π√(l/g) (small angle, light string, rigid support); g_eff = |g − a| in a lift or cart (a = acceleration of the support); very long pendulum, at most 2π√(R/g) ≈ 84.6 min (R = earth's radius)
• Physical pendulum 2π√(I/mgd) (rigid body, fixed horizontal axis, small angle, d = pivot to centre of mass), torsional 2π√(I/C) (elastic limit, C = torsion constant), U-tube 2π√(L/2g) (uniform tube, non-viscous liquid, L = column length), tunnel through the earth 2π√(R/g) (uniform earth, smooth tunnel)
• From U(x): ω² = U''(x₀)/m (small oscillations about a stable equilibrium x₀); superposition, Lissajous figures
• Damped amplitude A₀e^(−bt/2m) (damping force −bv, light damping b < 2√(km)); forced oscillations: largest amplitude when the driving ω_d ≈ ω (light damping)
