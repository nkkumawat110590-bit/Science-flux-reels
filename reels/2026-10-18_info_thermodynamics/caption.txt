• Zeroth law, heat, work W = ∫P dV (quasi-static), internal energy ΔU = nC_VΔT (ideal gas, any process)
• First law Q = ΔU + W with W = work done BY the gas; work = area under the P–V curve; cyclic process: ΔU = 0, W = enclosed area (clockwise on P–V means W > 0)
• Isochoric, isobaric, isothermal W = nRT ln(V₂/V₁) and reversible adiabatic PV^γ = const, TV^(γ−1) = const (ideal gas); adiabatic slope = γ × isothermal slope at the same point; B_T = P, B_ad = γP
• Polytropic PV^x = const: C = C_V + R/(1 − x); Mayer C_P − C_V = R (ideal gas); γ = 5/3 (monatomic), 7/5 (diatomic or linear, rigid), 4/3 (non-linear polyatomic, rigid); rigid = no vibration, ordinary temperature; γ of a mixture
• P–V, V–T, P–T graphs, free expansion into vacuum in an insulated container (Q = 0, W = 0, T constant for an ideal gas), heat engine η = 1 − Q₂/Q₁, refrigerator COP
• Carnot η = 1 − T₂/T₁ (reversible engine between two reservoirs, T in kelvin), second law (Kelvin–Planck, Clausius), reversible vs irreversible, entropy basics
