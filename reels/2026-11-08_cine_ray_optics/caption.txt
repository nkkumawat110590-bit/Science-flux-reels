Ray Optics ka POORA chapter, sirf 4 minute mein 🔭 Mirror se telescope tak, har ray diagram real equations se bana!

✅ Reflection, spherical mirrors, Cartesian sign convention, f = R/2 aur magnification
✅ Mirror formula 1/v + 1/u = 1/f, m = −v/u
✅ Snell's law n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r, n = c/v, n = real/apparent depth (near-normal viewing)
✅ Total internal reflection, n = 1/sin C: optical fibre, mirage, diamond
✅ Spherical surface n₂/v − n₁/u = (n₂−n₁)/R, lens maker 1/f = (n−1)(1/R₁ − 1/R₂)
✅ Lens formula 1/v − 1/u = 1/f, lens m = v/u, power P = 1/f, P = P₁ + P₂
✅ Prism: minimum deviation, dispersion (violet bends most), I ∝ 1/λ⁴: sky blue, sunset red
✅ Eye defects; simple microscope m = 1 + D/f (image at D); compound m = (L/fₒ)(D/fₑ) (final image at infinity); telescope m = fₒ/fₑ (normal adjustment)

Symbols: u = object distance, v = image distance, f = focal length, R = radius of curvature, n = refractive index, i/r = angles of incidence/refraction, C = critical angle, A = prism angle, δm = minimum deviation, λ = wavelength, D = 25 cm least distance of distinct vision, L = tube length, fₒ/fₑ = objective/eyepiece focal lengths, P = power
