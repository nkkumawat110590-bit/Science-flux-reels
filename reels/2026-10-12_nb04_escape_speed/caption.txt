Kya bhaari rocket ko zyada escape speed chahiye? 🚀 Notebook Proof #04

Proof (no air drag; Earth's rotation and other bodies ignored):
• Gravity is conservative → KE + U = constant
• Escape = reach infinity, where U = 0 and minimum speed = 0
• ½ m v_e² − GMm/R = 0 → v_e = √(2GM/R)
• GM = gR² → v_e = √(2gR) ≈ 11.2 km/s (from Earth's surface)

Twist: m cancels, and energy is a scalar, so the same v_e works in ANY direction (as long as the path does not hit the Earth). And v_e = √2 × orbital speed (7.9 km/s).
