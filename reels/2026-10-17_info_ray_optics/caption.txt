Inside:
• Mirror formula 1/v + 1/u = 1/f, m = −v/u (paraxial rays); f = R/2 only for paraxial rays, and a mirror's f does not depend on the medium; image velocity v_I = −m²v_O (along the axis, mirror at rest)
• Snell's law, apparent depth h/n (near-normal viewing), lateral shift t·sin(i − r)/cos r (parallel slab, same medium both sides)
• TIR only from denser to rarer with i > C: sin C = 1/n into air (glass 41.8°, water 48.6°); optical fibre sin θa = √(n₁² − n₂²) (entry from air)
• Thin lens 1/v − 1/u = 1/f, lensmaker 1/f = (n − 1)(1/R₁ − 1/R₂) (thin lens in air); thin glass (n = 1.5) lens in water f_w = 4f_a; P = P₁ + P₂ (thin lenses in contact)
• Silvered lens P = 2P_L + P_M (thin lens, paraxial), displacement method f = (D² − x²)/4D (D > 4f), prism n = sin((A + δm)/2)/sin(A/2) (minimum deviation), δ = (n − 1)A (thin prism, small i), dispersion, achromatic and direct-vision pairs
• Human eye defects, simple microscope M = 1 + D/f (image at D) or D/f (relaxed eye), telescope M = f_o/f_e and L = f_o + f_e (normal adjustment), Newtonian and Cassegrain telescopes
