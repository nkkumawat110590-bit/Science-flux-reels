Wave Optics: poora chapter, ek video mein 🔥 36 pages of revision notes + solved JEE Main and JEE Advanced level questions (Class 12, JEE + NEET)

Inside:
• Huygens' principle: wavefronts, reflection (i = r) and refraction (sin i/sin r = v₁/v₂ = μ₂/μ₁) derived
• Coherent sources: I = I₁ + I₂ + 2√(I₁I₂) cos φ, I_max = (√I₁ + √I₂)², I_min = (√I₁ − √I₂)², φ = (2π/λ)Δx
• YDSE: Δx = d sinθ ≈ yd/D, fringe width β = λD/d, angular width λ/d (D ≫ d, small angles); in a medium β/μ
• Thin sheet: shift (μ − 1)tD/d towards the covered slit; source slit moved by s: shift sD/b to the opposite side
• Thin film in air: reflected bright when 2μt cos r = (n − ½)λ (π change at the denser surface)
• Single slit: minima at a sinθ = nλ (n ≠ 0), central maximum 2λD/a, twice the others (Fraunhofer, small angles)
• Resolving power: telescope Δθ = 1.22λ/D_o, microscope d_min = 1.22λ/(2μ sinψ) (Rayleigh criterion)
• Polarisation: Malus I = I_p cos²θ (I_p = plane-polarised intensity on the analyser, ideal polaroid), unpolarised → I₀/2; Brewster tan θ_B = μ (reflected ⊥ refracted)
• Doppler for light: Δλ/λ = v_r/c (v_r ≪ c, receding → red shift)
Symbols: λ = wavelength in vacuum/air, μ = refractive index, v = wave speed, Δx = optical path difference (S₂ path − S₁ path), φ = phase difference, I₁, I₂ = intensities of the two waves, d = slit separation, D = slit-to-screen distance, y = distance from the screen centre, β = fringe width, t = sheet/film thickness, s = source-slit shift, b = source-to-slits distance, r = refraction angle in the film, a = slit width, n = order, D_o = objective diameter, ψ = microscope half-angle, θ = angle from the axis (or between polaroid axes), I_p = plane-polarised intensity on the analyser, I₀ = unpolarised intensity, θ_B = Brewster angle, v_r = radial speed, c = speed of light
